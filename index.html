What this site covers

Using historical reanalysis climate data (ERA5/ERA5-Land, via Copernicus/ECMWF), this project tracks how South Asia's climate has shifted since 1995 — annual temperature trends, precipitation patterns, extreme heat days, and a full seasonal breakdown following the region's monsoon calendar: Winter, Pre-Monsoon, Monsoon, and Post-Monsoon.
