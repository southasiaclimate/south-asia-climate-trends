3. Data accuracy and limitations

Climate figures on this site are derived from ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis data (via the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API), a scientific estimate that blends satellite, ground-station, and model data at a coarse grid resolution (approximately 9–25km). Reanalysis data can differ from official, station-based meteorological records, particularly in areas of complex terrain (as noted explicitly in reports covering mountainous locations). Trend rates are estimated using linear regression over the stated time window and represent a general direction, not a precise prediction for any individual future year.
