Every other city in this series is warming. Kathmandu's annual trend is
essentially flat — and two of its four seasons are actually cooling. This
explainer digs into where the divergence happens, what might explain it,
and how Kathmandu compares to Rasuwagadhi, a fellow Nepal location with
the exact opposite story.
