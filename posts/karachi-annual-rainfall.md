How much does it rain in Karachi each year, and is it increasing? The
trend is real — but it's not a steady climb. This report tracks the
actual year-by-year rainfall across 1996–2025 using ERA5 data, and shows
how a handful of extreme monsoon years, especially 2022, are doing most
of the work behind the long-term rise.
