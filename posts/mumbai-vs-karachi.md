Both cities sit on the Arabian Sea. Mumbai's rainfall trend is the
steepest of any city in this series — a broad, sustained monsoon
intensification. Karachi's rise is real but almost entirely a
handful-of-extreme-years story, dominated by 2022 alone. A head-to-head,
30-year data comparison of two coastal cities with very different rain.
