Islamabad's winter is warming at +1.05°C per decade — the fastest
seasonal trend of any lowland city in this series, and a complete
reversal of the pattern found everywhere else. This explainer digs into
why, including the honest case that urbanization and regional climate
change are both very likely contributing at once, and why winter
rainfall isn't rising to match.
