2024 was Delhi's wettest year on record by a wide margin (~1,200mm) —
but the city's fastest-warming season, post-monsoon, isn't getting any
wetter at all. This report tracks the real 30-year rainfall trend, the
last-decade average, and why Delhi's heat and rain trends are telling
two different stories.
