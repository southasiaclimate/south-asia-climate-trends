India's 2026 monsoon is running about 14% below normal through August,
alongside the country's warmest August since 1901 — exactly what WMO and
IMD forecast back in April, tied to a developing El Niño. But does a weak
monsoon year actually contradict the rising 30-year rainfall trends this
site has documented for Mumbai, Karachi, and Colombo? Here's what the
season-to-date numbers actually show, city by city.
