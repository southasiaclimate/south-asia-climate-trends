on decade-old, region-wide averages. This report takes a different approach
— 12 findings built entirely from our own city-by-city ERA5 reanalysis data
across Karachi, Delhi, Dhaka, Colombo, Kathmandu, Lahore, Islamabad, and
Mumbai (1995–2025), including a full 8-city comparison table.
