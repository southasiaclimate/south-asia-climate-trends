Dhaka is warming more than twice as fast as Karachi — but the rainfall
story flips entirely. Karachi's precipitation is surging, driven by a
handful of extreme monsoon years; Dhaka's is declining, despite
Bangladesh's reputation for flood risk. A head-to-head, 30-year data
comparison of two very different South Asian megacities.
