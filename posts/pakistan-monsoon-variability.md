Pakistan's monsoon season (June-September) delivers most of the country's
annual rainfall — and its behavior has direct consequences for agriculture,
flood risk, and water security. We used 30 years of reanalysis rainfall data
to look at how monsoon totals have shifted over time.

## Why this matters

Even where average rainfall looks stable, more erratic year-to-year swings —
some very wet years, some very dry — create real challenges for farmers
planning planting seasons and for flood preparedness, as seen in the 2022
floods.
