Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how South Asia Climate Trends ("this site," "we," "us") handles information when you visit southasiaclimate.github.io/south-asia-climate-trends.

This site does not require account creation or collect personal information directly from you to read its content. Standard web server and analytics logs may automatically record non-identifying technical information such as your browser type, general location (derived from IP address), pages visited, and time spent on the site, for the purpose of understanding site traffic and improving content.

This site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate certain features, remember preferences, and — where applicable — to serve advertising. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser.

This site uses, or intends to use, Google AdSense to display advertising. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet.

Key points about Google's ad cookies:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. For more detail on how Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Third-party links

This site links to external resources, including the Open-Meteo API documentation and an interactive dashboard hosted on Streamlit. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites.

5. Data source disclosure

The climate data underlying this site's articles comes from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5/ERA5-Land reanalysis, Copernicus/ECMWF) and does not involve or expose any personal user data — it is publicly available scientific climate data.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
