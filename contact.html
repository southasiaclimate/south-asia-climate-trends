What to include

To help get a faster, more useful reply, please mention:

  • Data corrections: the specific article/URL, the figure in question, and (if possible) the source you're comparing it against.
  • Methodology questions: which report or metric you're asking about — happy to walk through how a trend rate or seasonal breakdown was calculated.
  • Collaboration or media requests: a short description of what you have in mind and any relevant deadline.

Response time

This site is maintained independently alongside other work, so replies may take a few days. Corrections to factual errors are prioritized.
