About This Project

Data-driven climate reporting for South Asia

South Asia Climate Trends is an independent climate data project tracking how temperature, monsoon rainfall, and extreme heat have shifted across South Asia over the past 30+ years. Every article on this site is built from the same reanalysis climate dataset, analyzed with the same statistical methods, and written to make long-term climate signals — not daily weather — understandable to a general audience.

Who's behind it

This site is written and maintained by a professional meteorologist with an MPhil in Environmental Sciences and over 6 years of experience working in a national meteorology department. The analysis draws on hands-on expertise in climate data processing and statistical analysis using Python and SPSS.

What we publish

Each report focuses on a single South Asian city — Karachi, Delhi, Dhaka, Colombo, Kathmandu, and more to come — and breaks down three decades of that city's climate record: annual warming trends, extreme heat day counts, precipitation shifts, and a full seasonal and monsoon breakdown, following the standard South Asian meteorological calendar.

Data & methodology

All figures are derived from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, built on ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis data produced by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) through the Copernicus Climate Change Service. Trend rates are calculated using linear regression across the 1995–2025 window for each metric. This is the same category of dataset widely used in peer-reviewed climate science, and the same approach used operationally in meteorology departments across the region.

Why this project exists

Daily weather apps and forecasts rarely show the longer-horizon picture — how a city's climate baseline has actually moved over decades. That longer view matters for urban planners, public health officials, farmers, and residents making decisions that outlast any single forecast. This project exists to make that 30-year picture accessible, accurate, and free of hype.
