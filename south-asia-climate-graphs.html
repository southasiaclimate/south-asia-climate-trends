Mumbai Climate Graph

Mumbai's temperature graph shows a sharp recent acceleration, especially since 2023; its rainfall graph shows the steepest sustained increase of any city in this series (~+300mm/decade). Full breakdown: Mumbai climate trend report.

Delhi Climate Graph

Delhi's rainfall graph shows a dramatic 2024 spike — nearly double any other year on record. Full breakdown: Delhi climate trend report, Delhi rainfall, Delhi extreme heat.

Karachi Climate Graph

Karachi's rainfall graph is dominated by a single extreme year — 2022, roughly 27 times the 1990s baseline. Full breakdown: Karachi climate trend report, Karachi rainfall.

Dhaka Climate Graph

Dhaka shows the steadiest warming climb of any city in this series, paired with a declining rainfall trend — hotter and drier at the same time. Full breakdown: Dhaka climate trend report.

Colombo Climate Graph

Colombo is the wettest city in this entire series on a typical-year basis, with 2023 and 2024 both topping 3,300mm. Full breakdown: Colombo climate trend report, Colombo rainfall.

Kathmandu Climate Graph

Kathmandu's temperature graph is the outlier of this series — essentially flat, unlike every other city studied. Full breakdown: Kathmandu climate trend report, why Kathmandu isn't warming.

Lahore Climate Graph

Lahore's rainfall graph is nearly flat over 30 years, even as its extreme-heat-day count remains among the highest in this series. Full breakdown: Lahore climate trend report, Lahore rainfall & heat.

Islamabad Climate Graph

Islamabad's temperature graph shows the fastest warming of any lowland city in this series, led by an unusually steep winter trend. Full breakdown: Islamabad climate trend report, Islamabad rainfall, Islamabad's winter anomaly.

Rasuwagadhi Climate Graph

Rasuwagadhi's graphs show the most extreme trends in this entire series — the fastest warming rate alongside the steepest rainfall decline. Full breakdown: Rasuwagadhi climate trend report.

Data Source & Methodology

All graphs are built from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, using ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis data from the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) via the Copernicus Climate Change Service, covering January 1995/96 through 2025 (Rasuwagadhi's underlying dataset spans a slightly longer window). Dashed lines on each chart represent the linear regression trend.

Where can I find a climate graph of Mumbai?

Where can I find a climate graph of Delhi?

What does a climate graph show?

A climate graph typically shows how a location's temperature and rainfall have changed over time, often with a trend line indicating the long-term direction. The graphs on this page show annual mean temperature and annual total precipitation for each city from 1995/96 through 2025, based on ERA5 reanalysis data.

What data source is used for these climate graphs?

All graphs are built from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, using ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis data produced by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) through the Copernicus Climate Change Service.
